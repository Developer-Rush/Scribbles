School Uniform Suppliers Who Design for Identity

Scribbles began with a simple idea

Childhood is the most expressive phase of growing up and we believe it should be the best days of their lives. If children spend most of their day in uniforms, those uniforms should make them feel good. What started as a small initiative has grown into one of India's most trusted schoolwear brand, serving students who play hard, learn with passion and dream big.

Every uniform we create has the same goal:
Make students feel confident, comfortable and proud of the school they represent. We obsess over the little things; softer collars, better buttons, colours that stay bright, and stitching that survives every jump, spill and wash. Our fabrics are chosen with care and our designs evolve with feedback from schools, teachers, and parents.

Today, Scribbles stands for honesty, reliability and craft. But at the heart of it, Scribbles stands for children—their movement, their joy and their journey. Because a great school day starts with a great uniform.

Our Vision

To transform school uniforms from requirement to representation, so every student feels seen, proud and ready for the world.

Our Mission

To design purpose-driven uniforms that elevate comfort, identity and confidence through thoughtful craft, fit and understanding of real school life.

Our Purpose

To make schoolwear that students love to wear, parents trust and schools are proud of; combining design, fabrics and quality.

Why Choose Scribbles?

Comfort Designed for Children

Breathable fabrics, flexible fits and skin-friendly materials engineered for long school days.

Quality That Lasts

Stitching, durability, and colour integrity matched to global standards—built to survive every jump, wash, and season.

Modern Production, Zero Hassle

Integrated production, technology-enabled ordering, reliable delivery, and strong inventory planning—making uniforms stress-free for schools and parents.

Service You Can Trust

From custom uniform design to on-ground sizing support and retail options, Scribbles offers full-service uniform management.
