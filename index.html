We design and manufacture schoolwear that strengthens belonging, across classrooms, campuses and communities.

  • Build your next era of school identity

  • Design community-centered uniforms

  • Strengthen belonging across your campus

For over 18 years, Scribbles has established itself as one of India’s largest end-to-end school uniform brand. We have partnered with schools across the country to manage uniform programs that express each institution’s unique identity through thoughtful design and seamless delivery. Discover how a well-crafted uniform can reflect your school’s spirit, strengthen its culture, and create a lasting sense of belonging.

Uniform as Strategy

We shape uniform strategies that translate your school’s values into a clear, consistent identity experienced across every student touchpoint.
Uniform as Design

We design uniforms around real school life, balancing comfort, durability and form through thoughtful materials, fit systems and evolving design practices.
Uniform at Scale

We build systems that ensure consistency across production, delivering uniforms reliably across seasons without compromising quality or performance.
